# Skill: Project Research

Use when implementation depends on current external APIs, standards, platform limits, or third-party semantics that may have changed.

1. Prefer primary sources.
2. Record the exact external assumption that affects implementation or architecture.
3. Distinguish observed behavior from documentation claims.
4. When research materially constrains architecture, capture the implication in the relevant semantic model.
5. Do not turn temporary research notes into permanent architecture unless they encode durable constraints.
